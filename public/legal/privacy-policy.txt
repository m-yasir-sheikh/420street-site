Caird Capital Pty Ltd, ABN 30 693 472 534, trading as 420 Street ("420 Street", "we", "us" or "our"), respects privacy and is committed to responsible information handling. This policy explains how we handle personal information through our website, early-access registration, platform and related services.

We intend to handle personal information consistently with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles and other applicable privacy laws. Additional rights may apply depending on where an individual is located.

1. Scope

This policy applies to personal information about website visitors, early-access registrants, platform users, representatives and beneficial owners of operators and capital providers, professional advisers, service providers, prospective customers and other people who interact with 420 Street.

Business information that does not identify an individual may fall outside privacy legislation, but we may protect it as confidential information under our Terms of Use and contractual arrangements.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use 420 Street, we may collect:

- Identity and contact information: name, role, business email, telephone number, address, date of birth and government-issued identification details;

- Business and ownership information: organisation details, company numbers, directors, officers, shareholders, beneficial owners and authorised representatives;

- Verification information: identity-check results, sanctions and watchlist screening, politically exposed person indicators, fraud-prevention signals and evidence of authority;

- Licensing and compliance information: licences, permits, certifications, audit material, policies, batch or supply-chain records and regulatory correspondence;

- Financial and transaction information: financial statements, bank or accounting records, funding requirements, proposed terms, repayment schedules and transaction status;

- Platform information: account settings, access permissions, documents viewed or shared, communications, workflow activity and audit records;

- Technical information: IP address, device and browser information, login records, cookies, analytics and security logs; and

- Communications: enquiries, support requests, feedback, survey responses and records of correspondence.

We do not require tax file numbers, payment-card credentials or unrelated sensitive personal information unless a specific lawful need is clearly explained. Please do not upload information the platform does not request.

3. How we collect information

We may collect information directly from you, from an organisation you represent, from other platform users you authorise, from identity and business-verification providers, public registers, professional advisers, compliance or data providers, integrated software services, cookies and analytics technologies, and other lawful sources.

Where you provide information about another individual, you must be authorised to do so and, where required, ensure that the individual has been informed of this policy.

4. Why we collect and use information

We may collect, use and disclose information to:

- register interest, create accounts and provide platform features;

- verify identity, authority, ownership, licensing and business information;

- support compliance, due-diligence and fraud-prevention workflows;

- enable permissioned information sharing between selected users;

- support funding-request, review, transaction-record and repayment-tracking workflows;

- provide customer support and operational communications;

- protect users, systems and platform integrity;

- monitor, analyse and improve services, including through aggregated or de-identified data;

- manage billing and commercial relationships;

- comply with legal obligations, lawful requests and dispute processes; and

- send marketing where consented to or otherwise permitted by law.

5. Legal bases where applicable

Where a law requires a legal basis for processing, we may rely on performance of a contract, steps requested before entering a contract, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interests such as security and service improvement, consent, and the establishment or defence of legal claims. Where we rely on consent, it may be withdrawn, subject to lawful limits.

6. Permissioned sharing with platform users

Operators choose which information to share with selected capital providers, subject to platform settings and legal requirements. When a user authorises sharing, we make the selected information accessible to the authorised recipient for evaluation, due diligence, transaction management and compliance purposes.

Recipients may download or retain information where permitted. Removing platform access may not remove copies already lawfully obtained. Each recipient is responsible for its own information handling and any separate confidentiality obligations.

7. Other disclosures

We may disclose information to:

- identity, KYB/KYC, sanctions, fraud-prevention and compliance providers;

- cloud hosting, cybersecurity, analytics, communications, support and software providers;

- payment and billing providers for 420 Street fees;

- professional advisers, auditors and insurers;

- regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies or other persons where required or permitted by law;

- a buyer, investor, financier or adviser involved in a proposed or completed corporate transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality controls; and

- other parties with your direction or consent.

We do not sell personal information. We do not permit capital providers to use operator information for unrelated marketing or resale.

We may use essential cookies for security, sessions and platform functionality, and optional analytics or preference technologies to understand usage and improve services. 10. Direct marketing

We may send service updates and relevant business communications where permitted by law. You may opt out of marketing through the unsubscribe facility or by contacting us. Opting out of marketing does not stop essential account, security or transactional communications.

11. Data quality

We take reasonable steps to keep personal information accurate, complete, current and relevant. Users should keep account and business information updated and tell us if information requires correction.

12. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and the risks involved. Measures may include permission-based access, authentication controls, encryption in transit and at rest where implemented, logging, monitoring, backups, vendor assessment and incident-response procedures.

No online system is completely secure. Users must protect credentials and promptly report suspected unauthorised access.

13. Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably required for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining transaction and audit records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, preventing fraud and meeting legal, accounting and regulatory requirements.

Retention periods depend on the information, contractual commitments, legal obligations and risk. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it, subject to backups and lawful retention requirements.

14. Access and correction

You may request access to personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct inaccurate, out-of-date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading information. We may need to verify your identity. We will respond within a reasonable period and may refuse access where permitted by law, explaining the reason where required.

15. Additional rights

Depending on location and applicable law, you may have rights to request deletion, restriction, portability or objection; withdraw consent; opt out of certain uses; or complain to a regulator. These rights may be subject to exceptions. Contact us to exercise an applicable right.

16. Automated tools and risk indicators

The platform may organise information, apply validation rules or display risk and completeness indicators. Unless expressly disclosed otherwise, these tools support human review and do not independently make binding lending or investment decisions on behalf of capital providers. Users remain responsible for their own decisions and due diligence.

17. Data incidents

We maintain processes to assess and respond to suspected data incidents. Where a breach is likely to result in serious harm or otherwise triggers notification requirements, we will notify affected individuals and relevant regulators as required by applicable law.

18. Third-party links and services

The platform may link to or integrate with third-party services governed by their own privacy policies. 420 Street is not responsible for independent third-party privacy practices.

19. Children

420 Street is a business platform and is not directed to children. Users must be at least 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration.
